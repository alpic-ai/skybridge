Ask for something to buy and the model searches the catalog, curates the results, and renders its picks as a carousel. Tap a card to open a fullscreen detail with an image gallery, a variant picker, specs, and a link out to the store. A single `shop` tool handles browsing, search, curated carousels, and native product-choice forms. Full display data stays in view metadata while concise product facts ground the model.

## ChatGPT extensions

The source example also opens the Shop from ChatGPT's sidebar and conversation
side panel, and adds product mention search, native shopping preferences,
illustrated choice forms, and product deep links. Exact product variants can be
attached to the conversation with thumbnails and facts. Removing an attachment
does not remove the item from My Kit.

These integrations follow the
[OpenAI MCP Extensions specification](https://github.com/openai/mcp-extensions/blob/main/docs/spec.md).
Host capabilities determine availability. Use a compatible ChatGPT host to test
the native surfaces; local DevTools primarily exercises the view interactions.
